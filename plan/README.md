# Training plan

Put your current training plan here: a PDF, a screenshot, or a text file.
Claude reads everything in this folder. Files here stay on your computer
and are never committed to git.

To get a plan out of ChatGPT, paste this into the chat where you worked
on it, then save the answer as a PDF:

> Summarize my current training plan as a document I can give to another
> coach. Include: goal race and date, goal pace, my heart rate zones and
> max HR, current weekly mileage, and a week-by-week table from now to
> race day with each run's type, distance or duration, and target pace or
> heart rate. Note any injuries or things to be careful about.
